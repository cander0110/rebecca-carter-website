# Updating Your Website

This is your guide to adding and changing things on rebeccacarter.net yourself, without needing Connor (or any code). It only covers the day-to-day stuff. If something looks broken or confusing, just reach out.

## Logging in

1. Go to **rebeccacarter.net/admin**
2. Click **Log in with GitHub**
3. The first time, GitHub will ask you to approve access. Click **Authorize**.
4. You'll land on the content dashboard, with a list on the left: Paintings, Photography, Shop Products, Shows, Print Club Episodes, and Site Text.

You'll stay logged in on that device until you log out, so you shouldn't need to repeat this every time.

## Adding a new painting (with a price)

1. Click **Paintings** on the left, then **New Paintings** (or click into the list and hit the **+** button).
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — the painting's name
   - **Category** — pick `originals` for a one-of-a-kind piece (this is what shows up on the Works page and, if you check "Show in Shop" below, in the Shop too)
   - **Medium**, **Dimensions**, **Year** — whatever's relevant
   - **Price (USD)** — just type it like `$850`. Leave it blank if the piece isn't priced yet.
   - **Photo** — click to upload an image from your computer
   - **Availability** — `available`, `sold`, or `not-for-sale`
   - **Show in Shop** — turn this on if you want people to be able to buy it from the Shop page (leave it off if you just want it visible on the Works page for now)
   - **Description** — optional, a sentence or two if you want one
3. Click **Publish** (top right).

That's it. The new painting appears on the site within a minute or two.

## Changing a price

1. Click **Paintings** (or **Shop Products**, if it's a print/magnet/etc. rather than an original painting).
2. Click on the item you want to update.
3. Edit the **Price** field.
4. Click **Publish**.

## Marking something as sold

1. Open the painting (under **Paintings**).
2. Change **Availability** to `sold`.
3. Click **Publish**.

If it was checked "Show in Shop," you may also want to uncheck that, or just leave it marked sold. Either is fine.

## Adding a new show

1. Click **Shows**, then **+ New Shows** (or open the list and add a new entry).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title, Venue, Location**
   - **Dates** — just type it naturally, like "June 6-28, 2025." For a past show, the year alone (e.g. "2024") is fine.
   - **Start Date** — pick the first day on the calendar widget. This is what makes the little date badge on the home page work.
   - **Opening Reception** — optional, e.g. "Friday, June 6, 6:00pm to 9:00pm"
   - **Type** — Solo, Group, or Duo
   - **Status** — `upcoming` or `past`
   - **Description** — a sentence or two about the show
   - **Photos** — for past shows, you can upload a few images from the show so people can see the work
   - **Shop Link** — optional, only if you want a "shop this show" button
3. Click **Publish**.

If there are no upcoming shows listed, the home page automatically shows a "check the shop" message instead of an empty section, so you never need to worry about that.

## Everything else

- **Photography**: same idea as Paintings, but simpler. Upload a photo, pick which series/category it belongs in, and say whether prints are available.
- **Shop Products** (prints, magnets, ornaments, earrings, trading cards): add a name, price, photo, and description. If you've set up a Stripe Payment Link for it, paste that in too; otherwise it'll just show a "contact to purchase" button.
- **Print Club Episodes**: add the artist's name, a photo if you have one (otherwise it'll just show their initials), and their Instagram link.
- **Site Text**: three small blocks of text — the line under the hero on the home page, your bio blurb, and the footer's availability sentence. Edit these any time your situation changes.

## A few things worth knowing

- **Changes go live automatically.** Once you click Publish, the site updates itself, usually within a minute or two. You don't need to tell Connor or do anything else.
- **You can't break the site by clicking around.** If you're not sure about a field, it's fine to leave it blank and come back to it later.
- **Deleting an entry removes it from the site entirely.** If you just want something to stop showing (like a sold-out show), it's usually safer to change its status/availability instead of deleting it.
