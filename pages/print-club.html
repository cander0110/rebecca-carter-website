About the Series

Conversations with artists about their voice, process, studio, and upcoming shows.

Print Club is Rebecca's ongoing Instagram interview series, a place for honest, unhurried conversations with painters, printmakers, ceramicists, illustrators, and makers of all kinds.

Each episode explores the real texture of creative life: how artists sustain a practice, where ideas come from, what failure looks like, and why community matters. No prestige gatekeeping, just people who make things talking openly about the work.

New episodes every other week. Follow along on Instagram for full episodes, clips, and behind-the-scenes moments from each conversation.

How We Use Your Information

When you sign up for show notifications or apply to be featured on Print Club, we collect your name, email address, and any other details you choose to share in the form, like your Instagram handle or a description of your practice. This information is used only to respond to you and follow up about the show or feature you signed up for, and occasionally to send updates about new work or upcoming shows. It is never sold or shared with third parties. If you'd like your information removed from our records at any time, email visualartrc@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
