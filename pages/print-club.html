About the Series

Conversations with artists who make things.

Print Club is Rebecca's ongoing Instagram interview series — a place for honest, unhurried conversations with painters, printmakers, ceramicists, illustrators, and makers of all kinds.

Each episode explores the real texture of creative life: how artists sustain a practice, where ideas come from, what failure looks like, and why community matters. No prestige gatekeeping — just people who make things talking openly about the work.

New episodes every other week. Follow along on Instagram for full episodes, clips, and behind-the-scenes moments from each conversation.
