About the Artist

Making with intention.
Sharing with heart.

Rebecca Carter is a self-taught painter and printmaker whose practice grows from a deep belief that art belongs to everyone. Working across oil, screenprint, and mixed media, she makes images that sit somewhere between the intimate and the elemental — colour and texture holding what words can't quite reach.

Alongside her studio work, Rebecca runs Print Club, an Instagram interview series in which she sits down with artists, makers, and creatives to talk about process, community, and why they make what they make.
